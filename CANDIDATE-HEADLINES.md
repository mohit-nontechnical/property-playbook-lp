## Why these and not generic finance headlines
Property Playbook's audience subscribes for the honest, debunking take. An ad that promises
hype ("build wealth," "passive income that works") would mis-set expectations against the very
first post they read, so they would not open — which kills `acquired-open-rate`, the metric
`creative_equity` ranks variants on. On-brand contrarian hooks acquire subscribers who actually
open. Bonus: the "truth about" framing is also the safest on finance compliance (it debunks,
never promises).

Seed Creative Factory with **#1 The Truth About Short-Term Rentals** and **#2 The Truth About
Turnkey Rentals**. Both are broad, contrarian, on-brand, and compliant. Generate 15-20 variants

acquired-open-rate, kill losers.

## Copy guardrails (Mo Money voice + finance compliance)
- Lead with skepticism/truth, never aspiration or hype.
- No em-dashes. One emoji max (📘 only). Short sentences, concrete.
- No "guaranteed returns," "risk-free," "passive income made easy." Real estate is NOT passive.
- LP footer carries the "Not financial advice" disclaimer.
