## 4. Unit economics — the metric that governs everything

Front-end monetization is Boosts, so the headline metric is **net CAC**, not gross:

```
gross CAC    = ad_spend / new_subscribers
boost offset = boost_revenue / new_subscribers
NET CAC      = (ad_spend - boost_revenue) / new_subscribers
```

- **Goal at the front end:** NET CAC <= 0 (Boosts fully cover ad spend = free subscribers).
- **True profitability:** NET CAC < backend LTV (products + coaching + syndication value per sub).
- Rank ad creatives on **net CAC AND acquired-open-rate** — a cheap subscriber who never opens
  is worthless and never buys the backend offer.

### Boosts payout — KNOWN as of 2026-06-06 (from the beehiiv Boosts Marketplace)

Boosts are CPA: you recommend partner newsletters on your welcome/upscribe flow and earn per
qualified subscriber you refer. You can STACK multiple recommendations, so one new subscriber
can trigger multiple payouts. Boost revenue per new sub = Σ (acceptance rate × payout).

Best-matched offers for PP's real-estate/money audience (relevance drives acceptance AND
qualification — a relevant $5 beats an irrelevant $9.60):

| Boost | Payout/sub | Match |
|---|---|---|
| The Range Weekly Review (finance, wealth, $300k+ households) | $6.80 | Excellent |
| Strategic Trader (investing, finance) | $6.40 | Strong |
| No Commission Newsletter ("no agenda, no upsell") | $5.60 | Strong + voice match |
| Money Minute (business owners building wealth) | $5.00 | Strong |
| Exit & Equity (business, finance, real estate, "real numbers") | $4.80 | Near-perfect |

Skip for PP (low relevance = low acceptance): Threatstream/cybersecurity ($9.60), Machinery Brief
($8.00), HARD MARGINS/e-comm ($8.00), HR Jolt, Tuesday Briefing.

**Breakeven model (stack top 4 relevant ≈ $5.70 avg payout):**

| Scenario | Boost rev/new sub | Net CAC @ $2.50 Meta CAC | Net CAC @ $4.00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic (~0.5 accepts) | ~$2.75 | +$0.25 (≈breakeven) | +$1.25 |
| Realistic (~1.0 accept) | ~$5.70 | **−$3.20 (profit)** | −$1.70 (profit) |
| Good (~1.5 accepts) | ~$8.50 | −$6.00 | −$4.50 |

Realistic case = you get PAID to acquire subscribers before any backend revenue. Hitting it
depends on: (1) subscriber QUALITY — partners pay only for qualified/engaged subs, junk gets
clawed back and tanks acquired-open-rate; (2) relevance/acceptance of the stacked offers;
(3) not over-stacking (3-4 offers max, protect own retention).

## 5. Brand voice (verified from the live newsletter)

**Contrarian and skeptical. Sells the truth, debunks the pitches others sell.** Not aspirational,
not guru, not hype.

Defining pattern: **"The Truth About [X]"** — takes apart commonly-pitched schemes (Airbnb,
turnkey, ADUs, passive income) by reading the fine print and running the real numbers.

Voice traits:
- First-person Mohit. Honest about losses ("A $500k Lesson In Trusting Anybody").
- Plain English. Translates jargon ("The Capital Stack, Translated").
- Short, punchy, declarative. Concrete numbers.
- Skepticism as credibility: "the pitch sounds great until you read the city ordinance..."
- Self-deprecating, been-around-the-block, not a genius-guru.

### Mo Money voice rules (apply to all copy)
- **No em-dashes or en-dashes.** Use hyphens or periods. (Em-dash = #1 AI tell.)
- One emoji max, never leading. 📘 is the brand emoji.
- Avoid AI-tell phrases: "delve into," "dive deep," "in today's market," "it's worth noting,"
  three-part parallel lists, "leverage / robust / seamless."
- Short declarative sentences mixed with longer ones. Concrete numbers always.

### Finance compliance (this is finance content — enforce)
- Always visible: "Not financial advice. Do your own due diligence."
- Hard-banned: "guaranteed returns," "risk-free," "double your money," passive income framed
  as effortless (real estate is NOT passive income).
- Needs hedging: specific past-performance numbers ("past performance does not guarantee future
  results"), tax talk ("consult your CPA"), entity advice ("consult an attorney").
- No street addresses, no unit-level financials, no tenant details in public content.
- **Deal syndication adds securities-law exposure.** When syndication offers go live, get legal
  review (Reg D / accredited-investor rules, general-solicitation rules). Do not advertise a
  specific investment offering through these ads without counsel.

## 8. Monetization layers (the portfolio model)

Property Playbook is the template for a multi-newsletter business. Each newsletter can stack:

1. **Subscriptions** — free now; paid tier possible later.
2. **Boosts** — beehiiv's recommendation marketplace; CPA per referred subscriber ($4.80-$6.80
   for relevant finance offers). THE front-end breakeven engine. See §4 for the model.
3. **Ad Network** — beehiiv places sponsor CPC ads inside your sends ($1.20-$1.98/click; finance
   advertisers like MarketBeat, Morning Brew, Fintech Takes). Recurring LTV lever, NOT a CAC
   offset. Only meaningful at scale (negligible <few-thousand subs). Locked behind Scale plan.
   Do not upgrade for this until volume justifies it.
4. **Affiliate links** — relevant tools/services to the audience.
5. **Products** — digital products, templates, guides.
6. **Coaching** — higher-ticket, 1:1 or group.
7. **Deal syndication** — the long-term prize for Property Playbook specifically; investor
   capital into real-estate deals. Highest value per subscriber, highest compliance bar.

**Boosts vs Ad Network (do not confuse them):** Boosts = CPA, paid per NEW subscriber referred,
offsets Meta CAC at the moment of acquisition. Ad Network = CPC, paid when existing subscribers
click ads in your sends, a slow LTV drip. Boosts is the breakeven lever; Ad Network is scale-stage
upside.

## 9. Acquisition architecture

- **Creative engine:** Creative Factory (separate Vercel app) generates 15-20 ad-copy + image
  variants per seed angle. Each variant gets a `creative_id` that becomes the `utm_content`.
- **Launch + measurement:** Meta ads via Adspirer.
- **Landing page:** `~/Documents/property-playbook-lp/index.html` — zero-build, captures UTMs
  from the ad link and passes them through to the beehiiv subscribe redirect so attribution
  survives. Swaps headline by `utm_term` to match the ad (message match).
- **The join spine (non-negotiable):** `utm_content = creative_id`. Carries from ad -> LP ->
  beehiiv subscriber record, so spend can be joined to subscribers and net CAC computed per
  creative.

### UTM convention (every ad link must follow this)
| Param | Value |
|---|---|
| `utm_source` | `meta` |
| `utm_medium` | `paid_social` |
| `utm_campaign` | `property-playbook_YYYY-MM` (must equal the Meta campaign name) |
| `utm_content` | `{creative_id}` from Creative Factory (the spine) |
| `utm_term` | `{angle_slug}` from the table above |

> Beehiiv must be set to capture UTM parameters on the subscribe form, or the join breaks.
