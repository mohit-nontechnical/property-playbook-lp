The model, in three layers

The newsletter is not the business. It is the trust machine that feeds the business. Read top to bottom, then play with the sections below.

Layer
What it is
What it does
1
Newsletter self-funding
Builds trust at scale. Net CAC is negative (Boosts pay you to grow), so the list funds itself.
2
Education ladder $299 / $2.5k / $4k
Course, cohort, 1:1 mentorship. Pays operating costs AND qualifies high-intent buyers. These buyers are the bottom of the LP funnel, not the top.
3
Syndication the $1M+
Converts trusted relationships into AUM. Fees pay a salary; carry builds the net worth. Scales past $1M without more list.

Three numbers worth memorizing:  • Steady syndication fees/yr = capital raised × (acq% + hold × mgmt%).  • Carry per exit = equity × (multiple − 1) × carry%.  • Each qualified subscriber ≈ $300 of first-raise capital. A no-name guru is worth a tenth of that. Your 45-unit record and sales skill are the difference.
